# Acknowledgements

This site, **Sapphire Rapids**, has been built upon and deeply inspired by the groundbreaking innovations in the modern open-source software ecosystem. From lightning-fast static generation to sophisticated typographic interactions and comprehensive reading-enhancement toolkits, none of this would be possible without the generous contributions of the global open-source community.

We would like to extend our highest respect and heartfelt gratitude to the following exceptional open-source projects, their creator teams, and contributors!

---

- **Website**: [vitepress.dev](https://vitepress.dev/)
