Acknowledgements ​ ​

This site, Voile Magic Library, has been built upon and deeply inspired by groundbreaking innovations across the modern open-source software ecosystem. From lightning-fast static generation to sophisticated typographic interactions and comprehensive reading-enhancement toolkits, none of this would be possible without the generous contributions of the global open-source community.

We would like to extend our highest respect and heartfelt gratitude to the following exceptional open-source projects, their creator teams, and contributors!

🌟 Foundational Projects ​ ​

1. VitePress ​ ​

  • Team & Creators: Evan You and the VitePress Contributor Team
  • Website: vitepress.dev
  • Repository: vuejs/vitepress
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: The original source for our documentation architecture, typography, and content. VitePress has redefined modern technical documentation frameworks with near-zero runtime overhead and stellar developer experience.

2. Vue.js ​ ​

  • Team & Creators: Evan You and the Vue.js Core Team
  • Website: vuejs.org
  • Repository: vuejs/core
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: The progressive JavaScript framework underpinning the interactive UI and runtime components across the site.

3. Vite ​ ​

  • Team & Creators: Evan You and the Vite Team
  • Website: vite.dev
  • Repository: vitejs/vite
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: Next-generation frontend tooling providing lightning-fast Hot Module Replacement (HMR) and optimized build pipelines.

🧩 Knowledge Base & Enhancement Plugins ​ ​

4. Nólëbase ​ ​

  • Creators: 絢香猫 (@nekomeowww), 絢香音 (@LittleSound), and the community
  • Website: nolebase.ayaka.io
  • Repository: nolebase/nolebase / nolebase/integrations
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: Our underlying template architecture. Nólëbase pioneered the digital garden paradigm integrating Obsidian bi-directional linking with VitePress, offering plugins such as Enhanced Readabilities, Spotlight, Inline Link Preview, and Git Changelog.

5. UnoCSS ​ ​

  • Team & Creators: Anthony Fu and community
  • Website: unocss.dev
  • Repository: unocss/unocss
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: Instant atomic CSS engine providing high-performance, lightweight styling.

6. VueUse ​ ​

  • Team & Creators: Anthony Fu and VueUse contributors
  • Website: vueuse.org
  • Repository: vueuse/vueuse
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: Essential collection of Vue Composition utilities.

7. Markdown-it Ecosystem ​ ​

  • Key Modules: markdown-it, markdown-it-footnote, etc.
  • License: MIT License
  • Role & Tribute: Solid and extensible Markdown parsing and rendering capabilities.
